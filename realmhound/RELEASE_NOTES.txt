# Platform
* Added macOS support.

# Combat History
* New My Performance tab breaks down everything that contributed to your damage, with your stats and gear saved at the time of the fight.
* Fixed bosses sometimes shown as escaped even though their loot confirmed the kill.
* Fixed a stale local nexus marker on a fight completed by loot.
* The escape and nexus marker now applies only to the section it belongs to.
* Fixed dead allies being recorded as Escaped instead of Dead.
* Dead characters now show their last seen skin and dyes in Combat History and Loot History.
* Fight cards save dungeon mods along with the total Loot, Dust, and XP bonuses they granted.

# Damage Tracking
* Fixed items scaled by Vitality or Speed not counting toward your damage.
* Fixed ability damage sometimes being credited to the wrong ability after using another one.
* Fixed your own damage not including damage multipliers.
* Fixed longbows disabling damage tracking for the rest of the map.
* Fixed theoretical DPS calc crediting Savage with damage.

# Character and Stats
* Fixed Season 31 Crucible bonuses not applying on the Character Stats page.
* Fixed stale Crucible markers from the previous season on characters.
* Added Blood Ritual to the Character Stats page and to your own damage in Combat History.
* Redesigned character cards to show Blood Ritual and Crucible tags and bonuses alongside stats.
* Fixed the Legacy dungeon completion counter mapping on the Character Stats page.

# Loot and History
* Fixed loot boost timer display in character cards.
* Fixed reskinned Janus bags not linking to the fight where that Janus was killed.
* Reskinned Stone Guardians and Janus are now correctly labeled as Oryx's Castle and grouped into one card.
* Unified reskinned Realm encounters with their original variants in search and filters, so both names find them and the sprite matches the current live variant.
* Reskinned Oryx the Mad God 1 and 2 now map to Oryx's Chamber and Wine Cellar.

# Live Feed
* Added proximity-based Mysterious Crystal and Spectral Jailer entries to the Live Feed, with optional event sounds.
* Fixed Totalia the Malevolent not producing a Live Feed entry/notification sound when selected in the settings.

# Missions
* Repeatable missions that come off cooldown return to In Progress, and missions behind them stay unlocked.

# Sounds and Debug
* Raised the custom sound file limit to 15 MiB and added a warning when a file is too large.
* Added Shiny white bag, Shiny teal bag, and Shiny pet skins toggles under Settings->Sound->Loot bags, off by default.
* Added a Debug tab with a .rhcap recording toggle for testing purposes.
* Added app resolution settings for reproducing layout issues.

# Fixes
* Security enhancements related to API requests.
