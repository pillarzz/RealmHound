# Performance
* Reduced startup time, optimized Loot/Combat History searching.

# Combat History
* Fixed healed and phase-gated bosses deflating the whole team's damage through the overkill trim.
* My Performance card now differentiates weapon native shots, enchantment and other additional projectiles.
* Fixed enchantment sub-attack and proc projectiles missing from damage attribution.
* Fixed ability stat scaling not attributed correctly.
* Fixed ability damage sometimes being credited to the wrong activation.
* Fixed abilities scaling from MAXHP losing their bonus.
* Fixed healed bosses under-reporting damage after a mid-fight HP rescale.
* Fixed solo realm encounters reporting damage above the boss's max HP (e.g. Artificial Sprite God).
* Fight cards save dungeon mods along with the total Loot, Dust, and XP bonuses they granted.

# Character and Loot
* Fixed expired loot-boost timers still showing 0:00 on character cards.

# Vault and Treasury
* Items with empty enchantment slots stored in vault now show their true rarity and owned count correctly.

# Trophy Hall
* New "Show discovered item rarities" option that shows the highest discovered rarity gems in dungeon's collection (inside dungeon page).

# Missions and Quests
* Repeatable missions without a "(Repeatable)" title now show their cooldown and return to In Progress correctly.
* Added new settings for tracking mark progress and quest chests on Seasonal/Regular in Settings -> Taskbar & Quests.

# Chat & message notifications
* Added filtering by multiple message authors in the Name search.
* Added up to 20 custom Chat alerts, each with its own trigger words, volume, sound, and group name.
* Existing single custom Chat triggers migrate automatically into the first alert.

# Live Feed
* Fixed Totalia the Malevolent not producing a Live Feed entry/notification sound when selected in the settings.

# Settings and Sounds
* Added category-wide custom sounds for Veteran/Adept/Seasonal realm bosses and both enchantment categories.
* Entries still using the default sound inherit the category sound; separate entry custom sounds take priority.

# Items and Abilities
* Fixed Aeonic Talon and other items defined only in the supplemental equipment catalog.
* Fixed Teratoma and Stoneborn Husk ability data.
* Fixed supplemental catalogs wiping blueprint and ability mappings.
