Combat History:
- Local player damage now correctly accounts for temporary attack buffs (party buffs, consumables, proc enchants) and weapon damage enchants
- Rogue Lethal Strike now reads accurate: bonus side shots only count when they actually hit the target, and are no longer over-counted on multi-shot weapons
- Curse, Exposed and Petrified are now detected correctly
- "Hits landed" no longer includes shots fired at a boss while it was invulnerable
- Legacy The Shatters now tracks the opening phases of the Forgotten Sentinel and Forgotten King fights (Titanums/Obelisks, Royal Guardians and colored crystals), so leaving before the boss becomes vulnerable still creates a fight card tagged Escaped
- Legacy Lair of Draconis dragon fights now complete correctly instead of showing Escaped, with their chest loot linked to the card
- Bilgewater's Galleon no longer tracks the Galleon Admiral
- Cube Deity and Ravenous Rot add-ons are no longer tracked as separate entries
- The dungeon's main boss now stays as the card headline instead of optional side bosses or crates
- Search tags and Export JSON buttons now fold into a second row in the filter bar, making them visible on small screens
