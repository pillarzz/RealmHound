# Combat History
- Fixed some bosses sometimes incorrectly marked as Escaped when they were actually completed.
- Fight cards completed by a loot update now update in place instead of showing as Escaped until a reload.
- Fixed MV still marked as completed sometimes when the player nexused.
- Dead characters now show their last-seen skin and dyes across Combat History and Loot History, including the character filter pill.

# Taskbar & Missions
- Advanced dungeon variants no longer advance the base dungeon's mission.
- Multi-dungeon mark quests now fuse with matching missions when a dungeon overlaps.
- Two quests that share a dungeon now share a single pill.
- Improved tooltips for missions with multiple dungeons.
- Fixed character-exclusive missions to work correctly for Druid.
- After a season rollover, locked missions are no longer listed as "In progress".
- The season and battlepass countdowns now use the game's authoritative dates instead of estimating.
- Fixed dungeon callouts with dungeon names set to "none" to work with the auto copy-to-clipboard feature.

# Trophy Hall
- Legacy dungeons now have their retro collections, with a "Legacy Dungeons" filter.
- Redesigned the dungeon page with a header and a navigation row.
- The icon view shows per-dungeon collection completion with keyboard navigation.
- RealmShark imports now merge into RealmHound's dungeon rows by name instead of only matching identical spellings.

# Items
- New OWNED pill on item tooltips for blueprints, character skins, emotes and pet skins.

# Fixes
- Fixed gift-chest live updates in the Vault.
- The Season 31 shinies are wired into their dungeon collections.
